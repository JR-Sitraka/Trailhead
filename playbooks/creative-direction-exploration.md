# Playbook: Creative direction exploration

> **Status: PROVISIONAL-SPECULATIVE** — added ahead of any project
> observation. First candidate for removal if the next project never
> triggers it; must earn a retrospective mention to survive.

Load only when the optional `art-director` role is in play — after
product and UX context exist, before `design-system` starts. Never for
routine screens or minor corrections to an approved direction.

## Procedure

1. Translate product goals into three to five desired perceptions and
   an equally explicit must-not-feel list.
2. Record the functional visual constraints: density, session length,
   device, content type, accessibility, trust requirements.
3. Research references and decompose each into kept and rejected
   characteristics — a reference is evidence, never a wholesale style
   choice.
4. Produce roughly three directions that differ in strategic idea,
   hierarchy, typography, density, geometry, imagery, and motion —
   not merely color.
5. Generate comparable visual evidence for every serious direction:
   same representative content, same state, same viewport
   (`playbooks/visualization-prompting.md` governs the prompting).
6. Compare on product fit, audience fit, distinctiveness,
   accessibility, scalability, feasibility, and generic-AI-pattern
   risk.
7. Present the alternatives concretely so the person can react
   without inventing design vocabulary; record what to keep, combine,
   correct, reject.
8. Synthesize the selection, preserve the rejected directions with
   reasons, and stop for explicit human approval before anything
   moves to `design-system`.

## Output

`docs/04-design-language/creative-direction.md`, including the
approval evidence.
